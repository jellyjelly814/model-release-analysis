Zhipu AI (Z.ai)

We build the ChatGLM family of LLMs, develop LLMs as Agents, and release related LLM training & inference techniques:

  • GLM-5.1 & GLM-5, GLM-4.7 & GLM-4.6 & GLM-4.5, GLM-V, GLM-OCR, GLM-ASR, GLM-TTS, GLM-Image, CodeGeeX, CogVLM (VisualGLM), WebGLM, GLM-130B, CogView, CogVideo && CogVideoX. CogAgent, AutoWebGLM, AgentTuning, APAR.

We also work on LLM evaluations: LVBench, MotionBench, ComplexFuncBench, AgentBench, AlignBench, LongBench, NaturalCodeBench.

We started with social networks and graphs, and always love them: GraphMAE, GPT-GNN, GCC, SelfKG, CogDL. AMiner.
